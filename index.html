What Is Bathtub Reglazing?

Bathtub reglazing, also called bathtub refinishing or resurfacing, is the process of replacing a worn, stained or chipped surface with a new professional coating. The old finish is cleaned and prepared, damage is repaired, and a durable coating is applied so that the tub looks and feels like it was just installed. The same method works on ceramic tile, shower stalls, shower bases, sinks, vanities and fiberglass. It is not a quick coat of household paint. Professional grade materials and careful preparation are what make the finish last for 10 years or more with proper care.

Caring for Your Reglazed Surfaces

A little maintenance goes a long way. Once your tub, shower or tile has cured, clean it with mild, non abrasive cleaners and a soft sponge or cloth. Fiberglass tubs and showers are lightweight and common in newer homes, but their surface can dull, scratch and discolor over time. Fiberglass reglazing restores the finish without replacing the unit.
